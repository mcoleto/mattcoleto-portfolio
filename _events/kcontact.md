## Overview

KCON is the World's Largest K-Culture Festival, and KCON:TACT is the World's Largest K-Culture Online Festival! A total of 24 artist groups participated in the KCON:TACT HI 5 event held in September, which ran from the 18th to the 26th.

Across all five seasons of KCON:TACT, a total of 136 groups have performed, with 23 million fans from 153 different countries in virtual attendance.

## New Technology

KCON:TACT also introduced new technology such as MR (Mixed Reality), XR (eXpanded Reality combined with AR and VR), and mobile AR into the event as a way to enhance the audience experience for fans to maximize interaction with the artists.
