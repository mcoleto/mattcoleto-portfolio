## Overview

Going on its 6th year, Crunchyroll Expo is a premier fan festival that celebrates all things anime. Featuring unique panels, exclusive merchandise, and world premieres, **the 2022 show was both in person and streaming online August 5–7, 2022**, with an additional digital replay available until August 9, 2022.

## Notable Guests

- Hololive VTubers
- MAPPA's CEO
- Studio Bones' president
- Atarashii Gakko!
- Burnout Syndromes
- SiM

More info at [Crunchyroll Expo's official webpage](https://www.crunchyrollexpo.com).
