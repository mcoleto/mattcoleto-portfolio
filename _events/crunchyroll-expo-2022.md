## Overview

Now in its sixth year, Crunchyroll Expo is a fan festival that celebrates all things anime, with unique panels, exclusive merchandise and world premieres. The 2022 show ran in person and streamed online August 5–7, 2022, with a digital replay available through August 9.

## Highlights

- A hybrid format that let fans attend in person or join the full program online
- 70 featured guests, including Hololive VTubers Gawr Gura, Watson Amelia and Takanashi Kiara
- Studio leaders such as Makoto Kimura and Manabu Otsuka of MAPPA and Masahiko Minami of Studio Bones
- Voice actors, musicians, cosplayers and creators across panels and live performances
