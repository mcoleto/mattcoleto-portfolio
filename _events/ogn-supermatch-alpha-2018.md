## Overview

The first U.S. OGN Supermatch was held in December 2018, coinciding with the grand opening of the OGN Studio in Manhattan Beach, CA: the first dedicated Battle Royale studio in North America.

The world's top streamers competed together with fans in various Battle Royale games, all to benefit charities of their choice to the tune of $40,000 in total prize donations. The event featured the likes of deadmau5, The Try Guys (of YouTube fame), professional streamer JERICHO, the Cloud9 PUBG professional esports team, and many other gaming celebrities.

## Quick Stats

The event was live-streamed on Twitch, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook, and Douyu (in China), and generated over 6M views with 3.4M total unique views and over 540K peak concurrent viewers.

## Event Details

- **Date:** December 8, 2018
- **Venue:** OGN Super Arena (Studio 19)
- **Participants:** 4 groups of the world's top streamers competing together with 60 fans in various Battle Royale games
- **Format:** 4 streamers each led a team of 15 players, grouped into 4 different 4-person squads. 4 Battle Royale matches were played, with the streamers joining random squads on their team to lead them to victory. Points were tallied each round according to survival rank and number of kills.
