## Overview

On December 8, 2018, the first US OGN Supermatch marked the grand opening of the OGN Studio in Manhattan Beach, California, the first dedicated battle royale studio in North America. Top streamers teamed up with fans to compete in battle royale games, with $40,000 in total prize donations going to charity.

## Format

- Four streamer-led teams of 15 players, including 60 fans
- Team captains deadmau5, The Try Guys, JERICHO and Cloud9's PUBG team
- Four battle royale matches at the OGN Super Arena (Studio 19)
- Streamed on Twitch, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook and Douyu

## Results

- **6M+ total views** and **3.4M unique viewers**
- **540K+ peak concurrent viewers**
- **$40,000** donated to charity
