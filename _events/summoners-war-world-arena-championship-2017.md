## Overview

The Summoners War World Arena Championship is Com2uS's global championship for Summoners War. In 2017 I worked on three of its events:

- Summoners War World Arena Championship: LA Regionals
- Summoners War World Arena Championship: NY Regionals
- Summoners War World Arena Championship: LA Finals
