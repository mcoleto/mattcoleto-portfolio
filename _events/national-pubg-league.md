## Overview

The **National PUBG League** is the official PUBG professional league in North America. The league represents North America's portion of the 2019 official competitive season held by PUBG Corporation. The 2019 competitive season culminated at the PUBG Global Championship 2019, where teams from all across the globe competed for their share of the $2,000,000 prize pool.

The **National PUBG League** matches were played and broadcast live from OGN Super Arena in Manhattan Beach, CA.
