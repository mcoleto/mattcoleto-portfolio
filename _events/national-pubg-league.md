## Overview

The National PUBG League (NPL) was the official PUBG pro league in North America and the region's part of PUBG Corporation's 2019 competitive season, which ended at the PUBG Global Championship 2019 with a $2,000,000 prize pool. Every match was played and broadcast live from the OGN Super Arena in Manhattan Beach, California.

OGN's NPL broadcasts were part of the content program that drove **35M+ livestream views** and **4M+ VOD views** across OGN's esports channels.

## The league

Thirteen organizations competed, including Tempo Storm, Team Envy, Ghost Gaming, eUnited, Spacestation Gaming, Susquehanna Soniqs, Endemic Esports and Zenith Esports.
