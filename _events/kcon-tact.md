## Overview

KCON is the world's largest K-culture festival, and KCON:TACT became its largest online edition. When live events stopped, KCON:TACT brought the festival to fans at home with concerts and programming built for streaming.

Across five seasons, **136 artist groups** performed for **23 million virtual fans from 153 countries**. KCON:TACT HI 5 alone featured 24 artist groups over nine days.

## What made it different

- Mixed reality, XR and mobile AR staging designed for a screen-first audience
- A lineup that included Stray Kids, ATEEZ, ENHYPEN, TOMORROW X TOGETHER, MONSTA X, iKON and SF9
- A global schedule that let fans in every region watch live
