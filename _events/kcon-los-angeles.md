## Overview

KCON is the world's largest fan celebration of Korean culture and music. The first KCON was held in Los Angeles in 2012 and in the following years has expanded to New York, Tokyo, Bangkok, Abu Dhabi, and more, welcoming over 1 million fans worldwide.

KCON invites fans to learn and celebrate K-Pop, K-Beauty, K-Food and beyond through star-studded concerts, engaging panels, an expo overflowing with innovative products (and idol appearances!), KCON-exclusive programs, and of course artist engagements: your chance to meet and even hi-touch your favorite K-Pop stars.

## KCON by the Numbers (2019)
